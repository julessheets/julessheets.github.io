8 tabs in one workbook

  1. Start Here – plain-English set-up steps
  2. Settings – your names, due date, currency symbol, and editable one-off and monthly cost categories
  3. One-off Costs – a 45-item starter checklist (nursery, gear & travel, feeding, clothing, bathing & changing, health & safety, birth & hospital), room for 150 items. Each has a priority (Must-have / Nice-to-have), estimated cost, how you'll get it (buy new, second-hand, gift, hand-me-down, borrow or skip), actual cost and "Got it?". Totals for planned spend, spent, still to buy, must-haves still to buy, and what gifts and hand-me-downs saved you – by category
  4. Monthly Costs – 30 rows of ongoing costs with the month each starts and stops, projected month by month for 24 months, with year 1 and year 2 totals and totals by category
  5. Leave & Income – each parent's normal take-home pay, up to 3 leave phases (e.g. paid leave, unpaid leave) with months and income, and pay when back at work; plus normal household spending. See household income vs normal, the income gap, baby costs and what's left each month – and your savings runway (the month savings would run out, if they do)
  6. Savings Countdown – a suggested savings goal (one-offs still to buy + any shortfall in the first 24 months) or your own, saved so far with a savings log, how much to save per month and per week until the due date, projected savings and whether you're on track
  7. Gifts & Hand-me-downs – 150 rows: item, from, type (gift / hand-me-down / borrowed), value, date received, thank-you sent, give back – with a to-do for thank-you notes and borrowed items
  8. Dashboard – 8 cards (one-off costs, still to buy, year-1 monthly costs, income gap, saved so far, days to due date, save per month, saved by gifts), the savings runway message, 4 charts and quick checks
