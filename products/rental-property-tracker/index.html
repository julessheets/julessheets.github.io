10 tabs

  1. Start Here: plain-English set-up and tips
  2. Settings: currency, the month your financial / tax year starts, grace days before rent counts as late, lease-end warning period, maintenance alert, and 15 editable expense categories
  3. Properties: up to 10, with address, type, purchase date and price, current value, mortgage balance and rate, plus equity and loan-to-value (LVR)
  4. Tenants & Leases: contacts, lease start and end (or periodic), rent and frequency (weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly or monthly), bond / deposit, annual and monthly rent, and lease-end alerts
  5. Rent Log: 1,000 rows. Each payment shows Paid / Paid late / Part paid / Overdue / Due / Upcoming, with days late and balance
  6. Expenses: 1,000 rows by property (or 'General' for portfolio-wide costs) and category
  7. Maintenance: 300 jobs with priority, contractor, quote, final cost, status and days open, with jobs open too long flagged
  8. Rent Roll: every tenancy with annual rent, rent received this year, arrears now, late payments and last payment date
  9. Property P&L: rent and expenses by category for each property and the whole portfolio, net cash flow, equity, LVR, gross yield (on value and on purchase price) and annualised net yield
  10. Dashboard: rent received, expenses, net cash flow, arrears, late payments, leases ending soon, open maintenance, portfolio equity, lists of leases ending and tenants in arrears, and 3 charts
