What you get

Snowball or Avalanche? See both – and know the exact month you'll be debt-free.

The Debt Payoff Planner is a clean, simple spreadsheet that turns a pile of debts into a clear month-by-month plan. List your debts once, add the extra you can pay each month, and it shows you exactly what to pay to each debt, when each one disappears, how much interest you'll pay – and how much you'll save by choosing Snowball, Avalanche or your own order.

  • 1-page Quick Start Guide (PDF)

8 tabs in one workbook

  1. Start Here – plain-English, step-by-step instructions
  2. Settings – currency, first payment month, extra monthly payment, and strategy: Snowball, Avalanche or Custom order
  3. Debts – up to 40 debts: name, balance, APR, minimum payment, custom priority. Built-in checks warn you when a minimum payment doesn't cover the interest, when a minimum is missing, and when a debt is at 0% (so you remember the promo end date). Paid-off debts and blank rows are handled automatically.
  4. Payoff Schedule – a full month-by-month plan for up to 360 months: what to pay to each debt, interest, balance left, and a milestone marker the month each debt is paid off. When a debt is gone, its payment rolls onto the next one (the "snowball").
  5. Snowball vs Avalanche – side-by-side debt-free date, months, total interest and total paid for Snowball, Avalanche, your Custom order and "minimums only", with the interest and months you'd save spelled out in plain words, plus the payoff order and date for every debt
  6. Payment Log – record what you actually pay (and the balance from your statement); a progress table shows your balance now, % paid off, what the plan says you should owe by now, and whether you're ahead or behind
  7. Dashboard – 8 summary cards (debt left, debt-free date, countdown, % paid off, total interest, interest saved, Avalanche vs Snowball, next debt to clear), 4 auto-updating charts, a mini debt thermometer and a milestone tracker that lists every win in the order you'll reach it
  8. Thermometer – a printable one-page debt thermometer: fill it automatically or print it blank and colour it in on the fridge

  1. Download and open the blank planner in Excel, or click the included Google Sheets copy link.

  4. Follow the Payoff Schedule each month, log your payments, and watch the Dashboard and thermometer fill up.
