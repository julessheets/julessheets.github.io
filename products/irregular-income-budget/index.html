What you get

Income all over the place? Pay yourself a steady salary anyway.

The Irregular Income Budget Tracker is a spreadsheet made for freelancers, gig workers, musicians, creators, contractors and anyone self-employed whose income changes from month to month. Instead of budgeting from a pay cheque you don't get, you pay every payment into a "Holding Account", set tax aside first, and pay yourself the SAME salary every month. Good months build your buffer; lean months draw on it. Your salary then fills your budget from the top down – essentials first – so if money is ever short, only the least important items go without.

7 tabs in one workbook

  1. Start Here – plain-English, step-by-step instructions
  2. Settings – your currency symbol, start month, tax set-aside %, steady salary, bare-bones budget, buffer target and your spending categories in priority order
  3. Income Log – record every payment (date, client/source, description, category, amount), with an automatic income-by-category summary
  4. Monthly Budget – 12 months side by side: income received, tax set aside, salary paid, priority-ordered categories filled top-down automatically, plus an actual-spending section with overspend highlights
  5. Holding Account – your buffer tracker: money in, salary out, closing balance, % of buffer target and months of runway, with optional salary override and surplus sweep
  6. Sinking Funds – an automatic tax fund plus up to 9 savings pots (gear, holidays, car, annual fees…) with goals, progress and withdrawals
  7. Dashboard – income by month vs average, average vs lowest month, buffer progress vs target, months of runway, and planned vs actual spending by category

How it works

  1. Download the files and open the blank tracker in Excel, or upload it to Google Sheets.
  2. Fill in the Settings tab (about 10 minutes).
  3. Log each payment as it arrives.
  4. Once a month, pay yourself your steady salary and log what you spent.
  5. Watch your buffer grow on the Dashboard.
