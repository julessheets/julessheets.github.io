What you get

Income all over the place? Pay yourself a steady salary anyway.

The Irregular Income Budget Tracker is a spreadsheet made for freelancers, gig workers, musicians, creators, contractors and anyone self-employed whose income changes from month to month. Instead of budgeting from a pay cheque you don't get, you pay every payment into a "Holding Account", set tax aside first, and pay yourself the SAME salary every month. Good months build your buffer; lean months draw on it. Your salary then fills your budget from the top down – essentials first – so if money is ever short, only the least important items go without.

How it works

  1. Download the files and open the blank tracker in Excel, or click the included Google Sheets copy link.
  2. Fill in the Settings tab (about 10 minutes).
  3. Log each payment as it arrives.
  4. Once a month, pay yourself your steady salary and log what you spent.
  5. Watch your buffer grow on the Dashboard.
