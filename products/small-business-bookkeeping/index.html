What you get

Simple bookkeeping for freelancers, sole traders and side hustles – in any country, any currency, any financial year.

The Small Business Bookkeeping Spreadsheet is a clean, calm workbook that turns your income, expenses and mileage into a monthly profit & loss, a sales tax / VAT / GST summary, an income tax set-aside estimate and a list of unpaid invoices. Log each item once; everything else updates by itself.

What's included

  • Blank workbook (.xlsx) – ready for your own business
  • Sample workbook (.xlsx) – a full example year for a photographer & designer, so you can see every tab working
  • 1-page Quick Start Guide (PDF), with Google Sheets steps

10 tabs in one workbook

  1. Start Here – plain-English set-up steps
  2. Settings – business name, currency symbol, the month your financial year starts, invoice-date or cash basis, payment terms, your own tax set-aside %, a sales tax / VAT / GST on/off switch with its name and rate, filing period, mileage rate, and editable income categories (10), expense categories (20) and payment methods – sensible defaults included
  3. Income Log – 1,000 rows: date, client, description, category, amount, tax included Y/N, payment method, invoice #, Paid / Unpaid, optional due date and date paid. Tax and net amounts are worked out for you.
  4. Expense Log – 1,000 rows: date, vendor, description, category, amount, tax included Y/N, payment method, receipt ref and business use % (for part-personal costs like a phone)
  5. Mileage Log – 500 trips by odometer readings or distance (with a round-trip option), km or miles, optional rate per km/mile
  6. Unpaid Invoices – every unpaid invoice, most overdue first, with due date, days overdue and an age summary (not due / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ days)
  7. Profit & Loss – all 12 months of your financial year by category: total income, total expenses, net profit, profit margin, profit so far and a monthly income tax set-aside
  8. Tax Summary – sales tax collected vs paid and net owed, by month and by filing period (monthly, 2-monthly, quarterly, 4-monthly, 6-monthly or yearly), plus an income tax set-aside estimate you can compare with what you've actually put aside
  9. Expenses by Category – yearly totals, % of spending, number of entries, monthly average and a top-10 chart
  10. Dashboard – 8 cards (income, expenses, net profit, tax set-aside, sales tax owed, unpaid, overdue, best month), 4 charts, income by category and quick checks

How it works

  1. Download and open the blank workbook in Excel, or upload it to Google Sheets.
  2. Settings: currency, financial year, tax set-aside %, and sales tax on/off with your rate.
  3. Log income, expenses and trips as they happen. Mark invoices Paid when the money arrives.
  4. Check the Dashboard, Profit & Loss and Tax Summary whenever you like – they're always up to date.

File formats & compatibility

  • .xlsx – Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer (Windows, Mac, Excel online), Google Sheets (free) and LibreOffice
  • PDF Quick Start Guide
  • Google Sheets: upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and choose File › Save as Google Sheets (steps included). Sheet protection doesn't carry over to Google Sheets, so just type in the cream cells.
  • Apple Numbers is not supported.
