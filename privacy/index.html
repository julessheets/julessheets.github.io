Privacy

Last updated 19 October 2026

This website is a simple, static site. We've kept it that way partly so we collect as little information about you as possible.

What this site collects

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels. We don't run analytics scripts on this site.
  • No forms. You can't submit personal information to us through this website.
  • Fonts are hosted on this site, so your browser doesn't contact a third-party font service.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested, for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Buying a product

When you click a Buy link you leave this site and go to Gumroad, which handles checkout, payment and delivery (or to Etsy, if you choose an Etsy link, under the Etsy privacy policy). Gumroad collects the information needed to process your order (such as your email address and payment details) under its own privacy policy. As the seller, we can see the order details Gumroad shares with sellers, such as your email address and what you bought, and we use them only to support your purchase.

Links to other sites

Our guides link to outside sources such as government websites and research papers. Those sites have their own privacy practices. If you arrived from or share to Pinterest, Pinterest's privacy policy applies to your use of Pinterest.

Changes and contact

If we ever add analytics, a newsletter or anything else that collects data, we'll update this page first. Questions? Please message us through our Gumroad shop (open any product and use the contact option, or reply to your purchase receipt).
