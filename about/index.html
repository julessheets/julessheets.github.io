About Jules Sheets

Jules Sheets makes calm, minimal templates for everyday money and planning: budget spreadsheets, debt payoff planners, bookkeeping sheets, 2027 planners and calendars, and planners for big life events like weddings and new babies.

How our templates are made

We want to be upfront: our templates are designed with the help of AI tools. AI helps us draft layouts, formulas, instructions and descriptions much faster than we could alone. That's what keeps prices low.

AI doesn't get the final say. Every product is then built and checked before it goes on sale:

  • Spreadsheets are recalculated in a real spreadsheet engine and checked for formula errors. Each one ships with a filled-in sample file so you can see it working.
  • Planners and calendars have every date checked against the real calendar, and digital planners have their hyperlinks tested.
  • Printables are checked at A4 and US Letter sizes.
  • Listing images are real screenshots of the actual files, not mock-ups of features that don't exist.

Our free guides are written with AI assistance too, and edited for accuracy. Where we use a statistic, we link to the source we checked. Where a number depends on your situation, we say so rather than guessing.

What our templates are (and aren't)

They're planning and record-keeping tools. They're not financial, tax, legal or medical advice, and they can't know the rules where you live. For decisions that matter, talk to a qualified professional or your local government service.

Fonts and artwork

Our designs use the Lato typeface under the SIL Open Font License. Layouts, text and artwork are our own; we don't use other people's designs.
